Ha-Lan Nguyen (Alan)

AI Researcher · M.Sc. Computer Science @ VinUniversity

I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of latent reasoning, mechanistic interpretability, and representation learning for reasoning models. My research investigates how language models can reason through predictive latent representations, and how these representations can make reasoning more efficient. I am particularly interested in understanding the computational mechanisms underlying reasoning and in developing open-source methods that improve the capability, efficiency, and transparency of foundation models.

I am currently a master’s student at VinUniversity, where I conduct research under the supervision of Prof. Le D. Dung and recently worked on a project under the guidance of Prof. Trac D. Tran. Before that, I received my B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from FPT University and worked as an AI Engineer at Viettel Group, building search and recommendation systems for TV360, which serves over 72M users.

Research interests: Large Reasoning Models · Latent Reasoning · Representation Learning · Mechanistic Interpretability · Efficient Inference · Retrieval-Augmented Generation · Multi-Agent Systems
